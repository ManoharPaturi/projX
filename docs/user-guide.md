# OMR Evaluator — user guide

OMR Evaluator marks bubble answer sheets with a phone camera. It works fully
offline; everything stays on the phone. The same steps are in the app under
**Menu → How to use**.

## First time

The home screen shows a **Getting started** checklist. Tap each step:

1. **Add your institute name** — printed on every marksheet.
2. **Add your students** — *Students → Add student*, or *Paste a list* to copy
   roll numbers, names and batches straight from Excel / Google Sheets.
3. **Create an exam** — pick the answer sheet type and the marking scheme.
4. **Enter the answer key** — tap the right option for each question, then
   *Save answer key*.
5. **Print the sheets, then scan them.**

## Roll numbers and the check digit

Roll numbers are digits only, up to 7. Every student also has a **check
digit**, shown next to them in *Students*. On the sheet, students fill all 7
roll columns (with leading zeros, e.g. `0001234`) and then the check digit in
the last column. The check digit catches a wrongly bubbled roll number before
marks are given to the wrong student.

## Printing answer sheets

Open the exam → **Print answer sheets**.

- A4 paper, **Actual size / 100%** (never "Fit to page").
- Colour printing reads best.
- New printer, paper or photocopier? Run **Menu → Printer check** once.

## Scanning

Home → **Scan answer sheets**, choose the exam, and hold the phone above the
sheet:

- Lay the sheet flat in good, even light.
- Keep all four black corner squares in view.
- When the frame turns green and the ring fills, the photo is taken by itself
  (or tap the shutter). The marks appear straight away.

## Sheets to check

Some sheets need a quick human look — a faint mark, two marks on one
question, an unclear roll number. They wait in **Sheets to check**, and are
not given marks until checked.

Open one, look at the paper sheet, and:

- tap the answer the student really marked, or
- type the roll number if the app could not tell whose sheet it is,

then **Save**. Your correction is kept even if the answer key changes later.

## Mistake in the answer key?

Fix it in the exam's **Answer key** tab and save again. Every sheet is
re-marked automatically — nobody is scanned twice. The **Analysis** tab lists
questions that look like key mistakes (almost nobody right, or stronger
students doing worse than weaker ones).

## Results and reports

- **Results** — ranked list; tap a student for their answer-by-answer
  marksheet, to print or share.
- **Analysis** — class average, score spread, subject averages, hardest
  questions.
- **Reports** — class result list (PDF), Excel workbook, or CSV, shared via
  WhatsApp, email, Drive…

## Settings

- **Institute name**
- **Photo quality check** — *Normal* suits most institutes; *Strict* asks for
  sharper photos; *Relaxed* accepts softer photos but sends more sheets to
  checking.
- **Keep full-size photos for** — after this many days the large photos are
  deleted to save space. Answers and small previews are always kept.

## Privacy

Roll numbers identify students; names are optional. Nothing leaves the phone
unless you share a report yourself.
